Our story

Built on trust, restocked every generation.

Kakar & Lamba opened its doors in 1959 with a handful of household staples and a simple promise: fair prices, honest stock, and a shopkeeper who picks up the phone.

Seventy years on, that promise hasn't changed — only the shelf has grown. What started as a general store now carries everything from international skincare to local ayurveda, supplying retailers who've been ordering from us for decades, and their children who run those same shops today.

We still believe a wholesale relationship is built on the same things it always was: stock that's actually in stock, a fair bulk rate, and someone who remembers your last order.
